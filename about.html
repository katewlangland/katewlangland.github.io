About

I'm a Library & Information Science graduate student at Pratt Institute with interests in archives, art, rare books, and printed matter.

New York, NY / 2026—

Before beginning my graduate studies, I earned a B.A. in Literary Studies at Eugene Lang College of Liberal Arts, The New School, concentrating in nonfiction and poetry.

I'm interested in the ways books, images, and personal collections preserve creative and cultural histories. This website is an evolving record of my research, interests, and work.

Interests

  • Archives and special collections
  • Rare and out-of-print books
  • Artists' books and independent publishing
  • Photography, ephemera, and printed matter

Current focus

Studying Library and Information Science at Pratt Institute, with an expected graduation in Spring 2028.
